Why cold-pressed? 🌿

Cold-pressing slowly squeezes the juice out of fresh fruits and veggies instead of chopping them with fast-spinning blades. That means bright color, fresh flavor and every sip tasting like real produce.

Everything from Heather's Love Kitchen is made with organic ingredients and a whole lot of love. It's an easy, delicious way to get more fruits and veggies into your day. 💚
